FAQ
I have never trained before. Can I start?+

Yes. Most transformations start from zero. Your plan is built for your current level and progresses as you do. The only requirement is consistency.

Where do you train clients?+

Personal training is in person in Hyderabad. If you are outside the city, online coaching covers the same planning and check-ins remotely.

Does the plan include food and diet?+

Yes. Training alone does not transform anyone. Every coaching plan includes nutrition guidance built around your routine and food preferences.

How much does coaching cost?+

It depends on the format and your goal, so there are no fixed prices on this page. Message on WhatsApp, describe your goal, and you will get a straight answer.

How do I start?+

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page and say hello. Akash replies personally, usually the same day.
